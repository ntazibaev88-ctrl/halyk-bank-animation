## Motion graphics (not footage)

- National flag animation — generated in `gfx/flag_wave.py` from the public-domain flag artwork
  (Wikimedia Commons, *Flag of Kazakhstan.svg*).
- 1991 date card, 1992 UN card, 1993 tenge card, Astana line-art skyline, final title — original
  graphics in `remotion/src/Graphics.tsx`.

## Music and sound

- Original score composed for this film in `compose.py`, rendered with FluidSynth and the
  FluidR3_GM soundfont (MIT licence). The "dombra" line is a nylon-string guitar patch played in
  dombra style, not a recorded dombra.
- Steppe wind is synthesized in `mix_audio.py`.

## Voice

- Narration is **synthetic text-to-speech** (Microsoft neural voice `kk-KZ-DauletNeural`), not a human
  recording. `script.json` holds the narration, ready to hand to a human voice actor;
  re-running the pipeline re-times every cut and subtitle to a new recording.
