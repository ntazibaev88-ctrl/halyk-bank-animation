## What the film does not claim

- The figure **1,647 trillion ₸** ("approved 2025 budget revenue") from an earlier brief
  could not be verified, so it is **not used**. The 2025 plan was revised many times
  (925 bn initial project, 1 405 bn, 1 444 bn, finally about 1.83 trillion).
- The budget infographic shows example spending directions only, not their shares.
- The map of Kazakhstan and the Astana district map are stylised, not cartographic.
- The narration does not mention 2019–2022, when the city was called Nur-Sultan.
