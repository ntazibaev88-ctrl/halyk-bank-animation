Checked November 2026.

## Population (Scene 4)

- 2017: the city passed 1 million residents (June 2017).
- 1 January 2026: **1 538.2 thousand** people — Bureau of National Statistics,
  shown in the Astana socio-economic passport for January 2026
  (https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/documents/details/975994?lang=ru).
  The counter shows `≈ 1 538 200`, because the official figure is rounded to
  hundreds. The narration says "more than 1 million 538 thousand", which is true.

## Economy (Scene 5)

- **Gross regional product 2025: 18.9 trillion ₸** (preliminary). Reported by
  several outlets citing Bureau data (growth 11.6% year on year). It agrees with
  the Bureau's preliminary 2025 GDP of 159.6 trillion ₸ and Astana's 11.9% share.
  Labelled on screen as preliminary.
- **City budget: "more than 1.7 trillion ₸" actually received in 2025.**
  The brief asked for "1.647 trillion ₸, approved budget revenue". No source
  could confirm 1.647. The 2025 budget was revised many times (925 bn initial
  plan; later plans of 1 405, 1 444 bn and about 1.79–1.83 trillion). Reports of
  2025 execution give receipts of 1 777 bn and 1 863.7 bn ₸; both are above
  1.7 trillion. Following the brief's rule ("if a figure cannot be verified,
  omit it or mark it as approximate"), the film uses the verifiable claim
  "more than 1.7 trillion ₸ actually received", and labels it as budget
  execution, not GRP. To change it, edit `tools/narration.py` (s5) and
  `src/overlay/content.js`, then rebuild the voice and overlay.

## Tourism (Scene 6)

- 2025: **1 604 825** visitors, of which **380 301** foreign and 1 224 524
  domestic — Astana city akimat press releases on gov.kz (Astana Tourism centre),
  +7% vs 2024. These are arrivals (visits), not unique people; the on-screen
  source line says so.

## Landmarks (time-lapse order in Scene 3)

Baiterek 2002, Ak Orda 2004, Palace of Peace and Reconciliation 2006,
Khan Shatyr 2010, Hazret Sultan mosque 2012, National Museum 2014,
EXPO Nur Alem 2017, Abu Dhabi Plaza ~2018. The 3D models are stylised,
approximate reconstructions, not surveyed models. Scene 2 is labelled
"ТАРИХИ РЕКОНСТРУКЦИЯ · КӨРКЕМ БЕЙНЕ" (historical reconstruction, artistic image).
