## Facts checked

- 16 Dec 1991 — Law on State Independence of the Republic of Kazakhstan adopted.
- 2 Mar 1992 — admitted to the United Nations.
- 19 May 1992 — Nazarbayev’s working visit to Washington (archival clip).
- 4 Jun 1992 — current state flag adopted (shown as a symbol, never as 1991 footage).
- 15 Nov 1993 — tenge introduced.
- 10 Dec 1997 — capital moved to Akmola; 6 May 1998 — renamed Astana.
